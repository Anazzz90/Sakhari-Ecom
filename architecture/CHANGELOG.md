## 2026-07-30 — Architecture Readiness Review (ARR) Critical-finding remediation

A second, more critical Architecture Readiness Review (following the 2026-07-26 ARR remediation) identified seven Critical findings — two event-catalog gaps that left documented SRD states unreachable, two data-modeling gaps in the money side of the system, and three security-control gaps. All seven are resolved by architectural decision, not redesign: no new bounded context, no unnecessary module, no change to an existing architectural principle.

Authored seven new ADRs: **0035** (delivery-collected payment event — cash/card-on-delivery collection reaches Payment through a new `DeliveryCompletedWithPayment` event, forwarded by Order, never a direct Delivery→Payment dependency), **0036** (delivery failure and rejection events — `DeliveryFailed`/`DeliveryRejected`, closing the gap where Support already consumed `DeliveryFailed` but nothing published it), **0037** (Payment Ledger — an append-only financial ledger structurally mirroring the Inventory Ledger, closing the asymmetry where inventory had explainable history and money did not), **0038** (line-item aware partial refunds — a Refund now references specific order items, quantities, and the order's own Price Snapshot, supporting multiple partial refunds per order), **0039** (OTP abuse protection defaults — concrete, configurable rate/attempt/lockout numbers replacing an unspecified "Redis enforces rate limiting" statement), **0040** (step-up authentication for privileged roles — Super Admin, Operations Manager, Finance, and Support Lead require a fresh second factor before refund approval, permission changes, settings changes, financial adjustments, or sensitive audit access), and **0041** (store-scoped RBAC — authorization now evaluates Permission AND Store Scope, closing the multi-tenancy gap where a permission alone didn't confirm which store it applied to). `decisions/README.md`'s index and narrative were updated.

Propagated all seven ADRs into every document that had left the corresponding question open, modifying existing sections rather than duplicating content: `module-catalog.md` (Auth, Order, Payment, Delivery entries gain the new events, interfaces, and Payment Ledger/Store Scope Assignment ownership), `data-ownership-map.md` (Payment Ledger and Store Scope Assignment added to the ownership catalog and matrix), `capability-boundary-map.md` (new business events and processes for Payment, Delivery, Auth), `module-communication.md` (the checkout walkthrough, Example 1, gains the delivery-failure and delivery-collected-payment continuation steps), `service-decomposition.md` (new internal/application/domain services and repositories for Auth and Payment, new Delivery/Order published/consumed events), `data-architecture.md` (a new Section 8A, Payment Ledger, structurally parallel to Section 8's Inventory Ledger, plus a Payment lock-ordering update), `integration-and-messaging.md` (event-naming notes for the four new events), `security-and-compliance.md` (concrete OTP defaults in Section 4, a new Section 4A for step-up authentication, and a new Resource/Store Scope subsection in Section 7), `observability-and-operations.md` (Payment Ledger added to the restore-verification cross-check, new security-log categories), `nfr-matrix.md` (Security, Auditability, and Reliability sections, the Risk Assessment, and the Architecture Traceability matrix all updated), `DDD_Sakhari_Ecom_v1.0.md` (new entity 5.42, Payment Ledger; ## 2026-07-29 — Delivery Batching (Multi-Order Assignment) extension
Extended the existing Delivery module with delivery batching — grouping up to two (MVP) independent, same-store packed orders into a single rider trip — resolving the extension point both the DDD (Section 17.4, "Batch Delivery") and the SRD (BR-017) had deliberately left open in advance. No new module or bounded context was introduced; batching is a capability of Delivery, exactly as anticipated.

Authored **ADR-0034** (Delivery Batching), which records the decision and its two new Delivery-owned entities — **Delivery Batch** (batch-level status, assigned rider, store, timestamps) and **Delivery Stop** (an ordered pointer from a batch to one existing Delivery Assignment, with no status of its own — completion is always read from the Delivery Assignment it references). Propagated the decision into every document that models Delivery or the fulfillment capability, modifying existing sections rather than duplicating content: `DDD_Sakhari_Ecom_v1.0.md` (new entities 5.40–5.41, extended 5.22, resolved 17.4, new background job 15.8 for batch-formation evaluation); `SRD_Sakhari_Ecom_v2.6_Saudi.md` (new FR-BATCH-001–010 under a new Section 5.5.1, a new Section 10.5.1 batch lifecycle and configurable eligibility-rule table, updated BR-017 and BR-021); `module-catalog.md`, `capability-boundary-map.md`, `data-ownership-map.md`, and `service-decomposition.md`'s Delivery entries (new owned entities, public interfaces, published events, and — where previously missing — Settings added to Delivery's Dependencies, consistent with ADR-0033's already-established Settings-reader pattern); `module-communication.md` (a batching continuation added to the canonical checkout walkthrough, Example 1); `integration-and-messaging.md` (new events `DeliveryBatchCreated`, `DriverAssignedToBatch`, `BatchRouteUpdated`, `BatchCompleted`, `BatchCancelled`, and the new scheduled background job); `data-architecture.md` (batch-aware idempotency and named race conditions added to the existing concurrency strategy); `observability-and-operations.md` (two new operational-efficiency metrics); and `nfr-matrix.md` (driver/rider utilization documented as a monitored efficiency signal, explicitly never traded against an individual order's own delivery SLA). `glossary.md` gained Delivery Batch and Delivery Stop entries.
